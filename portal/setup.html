Salon Sang

iPhone setup

Three little things and your iPhone does the rest: it puts the portal on your home screen, alerts you about new bookings, and sends your clients' texts from your own number.

1 · Put the portal on your home screen

1
Open salonsang.com/portal in Safari and sign in.
2
Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow), then Add to Home Screen → Add.
3
Now it opens like an app, with your pink Salon Sang icon 💖

2 · New-booking alerts (free ntfy app)

1
Install ntfy from the App Store (free, by Philipp Heckel). Open it once and tap Allow for notifications.
2
In the portal, open Settings → Your iPhone and tap Alert topic to copy it.
3
In ntfy tap ＋, paste the topic, leave “Use another server” off, tap Subscribe.
4
Back in the portal, tap 🔔 Send me a test alert. A notification should pop up within a few seconds. If it does, every online booking will ping you too 💖

3 · Client texts from your number (Shortcuts)

A tiny shortcut asks the portal “any texts due?” and sends each one from your own Messages. Build it once (about 5 minutes), then it runs by itself every hour.

A. Build the shortcut. Open the Shortcuts app → ＋ (top right). Tap the name at the top and call it Salon Sang texts.

1
Search for Get Contents of URL and add it. Tap the blue URL and paste the Shortcut address (portal → Settings → Your iPhone → tap it to copy).
2
Tap the › arrow on that action. Change Method to POST. Under Request Body pick JSON, tap Add new field → Text. Key: p_token. Value: paste Your secret phone key.
3
Add Repeat with Each. It fills in Contents of URL by itself.
4
Inside the repeat, add Get Dictionary Value. Tap Key, type text. (It reads “Get Value for text in Repeat Item”.)
5
Add another Get Dictionary Value right under it. Key: phone. Tap its blue Dictionary and choose Repeat Item.
6
Add Send Message. Message: tap and choose the first Dictionary Value (the text). Recipients: choose the second Dictionary Value (the phone). Tap the › arrow and turn Show When Run off.
7
Tap Done, then tap the shortcut once to test. With nothing due it just finishes quietly. Now the portal's Settings shows “✅ Your iPhone last checked for texts today”.

B. Make it run by itself, every hour while you work:

1
Shortcuts → Automation (bottom) → ＋ → Time of Day → 8:00 AM, Daily.
2
Choose Run Immediately and turn off Notify When Run. Tap Next and pick Salon Sang texts.
3
Do the same for 9 AM, 10 AM … through 7 PM (12 little automations). Reminders go out about 4 hours before each appointment, never before 8 AM.

Keep your phone on and charged during the day 💡 If it ever misses a text, the portal's Today screen shows a Send button next to anything waiting, so you can send it with one tap.
