Caring for our seniors

As we get older, our nails and feet need gentle, expert care. Sang brings that care to retirement and assisted-living communities around Eugene, helping elderly residents feel comfortable, cared for and beautiful.

  • Nail trimming
  • Cuticle care
  • Foot scrub & cleaning
  • Gentle nail & foot care

A mobile service for senior living facilities. Family members and activity coordinators are welcome to reach out to arrange visits.
